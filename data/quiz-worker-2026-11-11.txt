Answer: Google - General Knowledge. Itha kelvi Star pathi - Answer Google. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Star pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Star la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Star oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
Q+A Katha - Moon 🧠

Question: What was the name given to Japanese military dictators who ruled the country through the 12th and 19th Century?

Answer: Shogun - General Knowledge. Itha kelvi Moon pathi - Answer Shogun. Moon pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Moon pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Moon oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Moon ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Moon la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
