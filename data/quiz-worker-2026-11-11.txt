Q+A Katha - Egypt 🧠

Question: In the novel "Lord of the Rings", how many rings of power were given to the race of man?

Answer: 9 - Entertainment: Books. Itha kelvi Egypt pathi - Answer 9. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Egypt pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Egypt la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Egypt oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
